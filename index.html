Vote on April 7th, 2026!

Working to make Eau Claire a better place to live has been a passion of mine for over 20 years. I have had the pleasure of organizing alongside many great people who’ve helped get us to where we are today. I feel I’ve become a leader who listens and acts upon the ambitions of this community, and I have a track record of helping citizens get involved and make a difference.

As a member of the Eau Claire City Council between 2018-2024, representing District 3 for two terms, I worked to make sure that more people have a voice in our local government, by supporting the creation of the public comment period at all City Council meetings, and at boards, committees, and commissions. I want to help bring public engagement to the next level as City Council President, because we can all help to solve problems and take care of each other and our city.

Before joining the City Council, I helped to create the Chippewa Valley Bike Map (2015), the City of Eau Claire Sustainability Advisory Committee (2014), and expanded community gardens and bike and walking trails throughout the city. Since 2022, I have been working as a community organizer for Citizen Action of Wisconsin, fighting on the state and national level for access to affordable healthcare, and to tackle the climate crisis.

I would appreciate your vote for Eau Claire City Council President on Tuesday, April 7th!
